## International IP standards overview

TRIPS is Annex 1C to the Marrakesh Agreement Establishing the World Trade Organization. Its standards address copyright and related rights, trade marks, geographical indications, industrial designs, patents, layout-designs, undisclosed information, and certain licensing practices.

For product-routing purposes, TRIPS supplies international baseline obligations for WTO members; it does not itself replace the national application, registration, examination, or enforcement rules in a target market. A product intended for export therefore requires checking the relevant destination-country regime in addition to Indian law.
