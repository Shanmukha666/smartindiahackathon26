Section 3(p) Traditional Knowledge Assessment

High Scrutiny Alert

The Indian Patents Act 1970 explicitly bars inventions that are essentially “an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known component(s)” under Section 3(p). Turmeric (Curcuma longa, Haridra) combined with Black Pepper (Piper nigrum / Piperine, Maricha) is an established Trikatu/classical combination extensively documented across Ayurvedic texts (Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita) for digestive and bio-enhancing actions.

gavel Statutory Threshold: To overcome Section 3(p), you cannot claim the systemic efficacy of the herb duo itself. You must limit independent claims solely to the thermodynamic liposomal carrier platform, demonstrating comparative synergistic data surpassing mere physical aggregation.

Biological Diversity Act (BDA) 2002 Mandate

Section 6 Pre-Requisite

Under Section 6(1) of the Biological Diversity Act 2002, no person or corporate entity may apply for any intellectual property right in India or abroad for an invention based on any biological resource obtained from India without obtaining prior approval of the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA).
