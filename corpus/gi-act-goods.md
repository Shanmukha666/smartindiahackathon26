## Section 2(e) - goods

goods means any agricultural, natural or manufactured goods or any goods of handicraft or of industry and includes food stuff.

A geographical indication identifies goods as originating in, or manufactured in, the territory of a country, or a region or locality in that territory, where a given quality, reputation or other characteristic of the goods is essentially attributable to its geographical origin.
