## Formulation categories

The standards of quality for drugs are prescribed in the Second Schedule. A drug is deemed to be of standard quality when it complies with the requirements of the relevant pharmacopoeial standard or, where no such standard is prescribed, with the standard specified for that drug.

The Act and rules distinguish medicines and preparations by their composition, intended use, route of administration, and the standards applicable to their formulation. The applicable monograph, label, and manufacturing requirements must be checked for the relevant category.
