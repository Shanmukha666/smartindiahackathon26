## Industrial design registration overview

The Designs Act, 2000 addresses protection for features of shape, configuration, pattern, ornament, or composition of lines or colours applied to an article through an industrial process, where those features appeal to and are judged solely by the eye.

For product-routing purposes, design protection concerns the visual appearance of an article rather than its functional principle, brand identity, or the patentability of a formulation. The Act restricts registration of designs that are not new or original, have been previously published, are not distinguishable from known designs, or contain specified prohibited matter.
