## Section 21 - Determination of equitable benefit sharing

The National Biodiversity Authority shall while granting approval under section 19 or section 20 ensure that the terms and conditions subject to which approval is granted secure an equitable share in the benefits arising out of the use of accessed biological resources, their by-products, innovations and practices associated with their use and applications.

The Authority may determine the benefit sharing which may be made in monetary or non-monetary terms, including technology transfer, location of production, research and development, and association of Indian scientists and local people with research and development.
